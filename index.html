Quick navigation

  1. Open the Vaultline dashboard
  2. Register your account
  3. Check your email for verification
  4. Activate your account from email
  5. Open Configurator → Accounts
  6. Start adding a trading account
  7. Enter MT4 or MT5 credentials
  8. Confirm account is added
  9. Open Trade Copiers
  10. Create a copier
  11. Configure copier settings pick broker
  12. Open copier settings
  13. Enable copier mode
  14. Dashboard — accounts connected
1

Open the Vaultline dashboard

  1. Go to dashboard.vaultline.co.
  2. Click Signup in the top-right corner.
2

Register your account

  1. Enter Full Name, Email Address, Password, and Password confirmation.
  2. Click Sign up.
3

Check your email for verification

  1. Open your inbox for the same email used in Step 2.
  2. If no email arrives, click Resend verification email.
4

Activate your account from email

  1. Open the Vaultline verification email.
  2. Click Verify Account.
Mandatory checkpoint: After Step 4, client must confirm with their account specialist that backend approval and rollover are completed before continuing.
5

Open Configurator Accounts

  1. In the left menu, open Configurator.
  2. Click Accounts.
6

Start adding a trading account

  1. On the Accounts page, click + Add Account.
7

Enter MT4 or MT5 credentials

  1. Select platform (MetaTrader 4 or MetaTrader 5).
  2. Fill in account details (name, account number, password, broker, server).
  3. Click Add Account.
8

Confirm account is added

  1. Verify the new account appears in the accounts table.
9

Open Trade Copiers

  1. In the left menu under Configurator, click Trade Copiers.
10

Create a copier

  1. On the Trade Copiers page, click Create Copier.
11

Configure copier settings

  1. Set Copy from to the Strata Ecosystem account that matches the client’s broker (see table below).
  2. Set Send to as the client’s own trading account (added in Step 7).
  3. Set risk settings and options as shown.
  4. Tick I have read the T&C’s, then click Create Copier.

12

Open copier settings

  1. Find the created copier in the list.
  2. Click the settings/gear button on that row.
13

Enable copier mode

  1. In General, set Copier Mode to ON.
  2. Click Update.
Check before finishing — does this broker need symbol mapping?
  • Confirm with the account specialist whether the client’s broker (OX, GNT, Knight Markets or other) needs symbol mapping.
  • If it does, the account specialist will handle the mapping setup with the client. If not, go straight to Step 14.
14

Finish and return to dashboard

  1. Click Dashboard in the left menu to return to the main page.
  2. On the Dashboard, you should see Strata’s account and your trading account connected. Onboarding is complete.
